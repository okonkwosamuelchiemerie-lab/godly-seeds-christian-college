Our history

Godly Seeds Christian College was founded on the belief that every child is a seed worth planting well. What began as a small classroom of dedicated teachers has grown into a full secondary school with modern laboratories, a library and a strong record in external examinations.


Through every stage of growth, the school has kept one aim: to raise students who are academically sound, morally upright and ready to serve their communities.
